# UX research: Planner 5D (observed 2026-10-31)

Directly observed in the web editor (free account, blank "From scratch" project). Used for interaction ideas only; no branding, assets, or code are copied.

## Observed
- **Floating chrome over a full-bleed canvas.** No fixed side panels: top bar is a row of rounded pill groups (back/project name, layers, 2D|3D toggle, AI, snapshot, comments, add, settings), a vertical icon rail on the left (search, construction, furniture, outdoor, favorites, new, custom items, shop), undo/redo bottom-left with a grid toggle, zoom +/−/recenter/help bottom-right.
- **2D/3D segmented toggle** is the most prominent control, top center.
- **2D plan:** room drawn with a thick dark wall outline, dimension lines with tick marks on all four sides ("5 m"), centered area label ("Room (24.980 m2)").
- **Catalog drawer** slides out from the rail: search with image-search, tabs (Rooms / Categories / My Items), category cards with photos (Bedroom → Double beds, Single beds, Dressers, Stands…), then product thumbnails.
- **Click to place:** clicking a thumbnail drops the item at the room center and selects it immediately.
- **Selection:** green outline. A **floating property bar** appears bottom-center with Width, Depth, Height (cm), "Levitation" (elevation), Angle — each a compact field with a drag/scrub handle.
- **3D view:** high three-quarter camera looking into the room; walls rendered with a dark cap on top, white interiors, wood floor texture, exterior brick.
- Right-click on an object showed no context menu (it deselected).

## Takeaways applied to Reality Builder
1. Selected-object quick bar at bottom-center of the viewport (W/D/H/elevation/angle) in addition to the inspector.
2. Dimension lines + area label on the room.
3. Dark wall caps / light interior walls / warm floor; three-quarter default camera that frames the whole room.
4. Place-and-select from the library (already done); catalog grouped by room/category.
5. Keep our fixed inspector: Reality Builder's validation explanations need persistent space that Planner 5D's floating bar does not have.
